privacy :: policy

Your photos stay on your device — unless you ask them not to.

Most tools that touch your images upload them to a server before you’ve done anything. instagrid doesn’t. Everything — importing, cropping, arranging, captioning, exporting — happens inside your browser, on your machine, and nothing is transmitted anywhere. The single exception is shared boards: if you deliberately create one so you can carry a grid to your phone, your photos are uploaded to private storage. That is opt-in, it is off until you press the button, and this page tells you exactly what it does.

LOCAL_BY_DEFAULT

Every action — importing, cropping, arranging, captioning, previewing, exporting — runs locally in your browser. Until and unless you create a shared board, no image is ever transmitted anywhere.

SHARED_BOARDS

The SYNC button creates a board so the same grid opens on your other devices. Here is precisely what changes when you use it:

  • Your photos are uploaded to a private object store and can only be read back through this site’s own API, using the board secret.
  • Your layout, captions and profile mockup are stored alongside them as a small JSON file.
  • The board is identified by a secret link. Anyone holding that link can read and edit the board — treat it like a password, and don’t post it anywhere public.
  • The secret lives in the URL fragment, which browsers never send to a server, so it stays out of access logs. It is stored only as a hash on our side.
  • If a link leaks, Rotate secret invalidates it everywhere. Stop syncing here detaches this device and leaves your local copy intact.
  • Your local copy always remains the working copy, so the app keeps working offline and a backend outage costs you nothing.

No shared board is ever created for you automatically, and creating one does not add an account, a cookie, or any tracking.

NO_ACCOUNT

No signup. No login. No email required. Open the page and start planning. We never ask who you are because we never need to know.

NO_COOKIES

No cookies — not even "necessary" ones. The app works fine without them.

NO_ANALYTICS

No Google Analytics, no heatmaps, no A/B tests, no telemetry, no "anonymous usage data." We have no idea how you got here or what you tap. That's the point.

NO_THIRD_PARTY_SCRIPTS

No external JavaScript, no tracking pixels, no social widgets phoning home. The entire tool is one file you can read top to bottom, fork, and self-host.

LOCAL_STORAGE

So your work is still there when you come back, instagrid saves it in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). It lives on your device. Clear your browser data and it's gone. If you've created a shared board, a copy also lives in your private storage until you delete it there.

SERVER_LOGS

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps basic access logs (IP, timestamp, path, user agent) for security and operations. Board secrets never appear in those logs: they travel in the URL fragment, which browsers do not transmit, and in a request header rather than a query string. instagrid itself records nothing about you.

WHAT_WE_DONT_DO

  • Upload your photos anywhere unless you create a shared board
  • Look at the contents of a shared board
  • Sell or share your data
  • Show you ads or retarget you
  • Build behavioral profiles
  • Track you across websites

EXTERNAL_LINKS

We link out to GitHub, Instagram, and chrisvrakas.com. Once you click, you're under their privacy policies, not ours. We can't control what they do.

YOUR_RIGHTS

Under GDPR, CCPA, and basic decency you have the right to know what data we hold, to delete it, and to take a copy. The short version: you're opted out by default, because we collect nothing to begin with. If you created a shared board, the only data held is the content you put in it, reachable solely through your own secret link.

ENCRYPTION

Served over HTTPS (TLS). Your connection is encrypted. For maximum privacy, consider Tor Browser or a VPN — if Cloudflare never sees your real IP, neither does anyone else.

LEGAL_REQUESTS

We may be legally required to comply with valid requests from authorities. We collect nothing about you, so for local-only use there is nothing to hand over. Shared-board content is the one exception, and it exists only because you chose to create one.

PHILOSOPHY

Privacy is a fundamental right — it isn't about having something to hide, it's about having everything to protect. The lie the internet sold you is that privacy costs convenience. instagrid is the counter-evidence: no account, nothing uploaded by default, no waiting — and it's faster and easier than the alternatives, not in spite of being private but because of it. If more tools worked this way, the web would be a better place.
