instagrid — a privacy-first Instagram grid planner

instagrid is a free, open-source Instagram grid planner that runs entirely in your browser. Plan and preview your Instagram feed before you post: drop in your photos anywhere on the page, arrange the grid, crop each tile with pan and zoom, and insert rows to push the whole plan down as new posts land on top. instagrid uses Instagram's real 3:4 thumbnail ratio, with 4:5 and 1:1 options for legacy content. Write and store the caption for each planned post, then export post-ready images as a ZIP — captions included as a text file — or export your entire profile as one image to preview the whole feed. Everything happens locally on your device by default: no account, no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. If you want the same board on your phone, you can opt into secret-link sync, which is the only time anything is uploaded. instagrid is open source under the MIT license, and you can read, fork, and self-host it.

TIPS
  • Add photos — drop them anywhere on the page. Drop straight onto a tile to fill that tile.
  • Rearrange — grab a tile's ⠿ handle and drag it to a new spot.
  • Reorder rows — on desktop, grab the ⠿ handle on the left of any row and drag it up or down.
  • Make room — hit ⬇ beside a row to open an empty row there, pushing that row and everything below it down. ✕ takes a row back out. The grid always keeps a spare row at the bottom.
  • Reframe a photo — drag inside it to move the crop; mouse wheel or pinch to zoom. On a trackpad, hold Ctrl while scrolling.
  • Zoom on mobile — tap a tile to select it, then pinch with two fingers directly on the tile.
  • Keyboard shortcuts — Ctrl Z / Ctrl Y to undo / redo. Delete removes selected tiles.
  • Export your work — hit EXPORT to save every tile as a ready-to-post image, or your whole feed as one picture.
FAQ
Do my photos stay private?

By default, yes — instagrid runs in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. Your images stay on your device. The one exception is if you choose to create a shared board from the SYNC button: that uploads your photos to private storage so your other devices can load them, and anyone holding the board link can view and edit it. Until you do that, nothing leaves your device.

How do captions work?

Click the ✎ on any tile to write the caption for that post, with live counts against Instagram's 2,200-character and 30-hashtag limits. Captions are attached to the grid slot, so they travel with a tile when you rearrange the feed. The editor has Copy caption to put the text on your clipboard and Download original to pull the photo back out as you imported it, uncropped. For the post-ready 1080px crop, use EXPORT — a ZIP there includes a captions.txt numbered to match the image files.

Do I need an account?

No signup, no login, no email. Open it and start planning.

Is it really free?

Yes — free and open-source (MIT). No ads, no upsells, nothing behind a paywall.

Where is my work saved?

Locally, in your browser — on your device, never a server. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Why a 3:4 grid?

Instagram now crops grid thumbnails to 3:4, so instagrid matches it by default. Switch to 4:5 or 1:1 anytime.

Is it open source?

Yes — one readable file. Read or fork it on GitHub ↗
